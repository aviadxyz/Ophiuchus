The mockup also lists six entries that name a body of work, not one paper ("review literature", "NASA Human Research Program: ..."). Its links for those go to a PubMed search or to the front page of NASA's Human Research Roadmap. They have no single correct address, so they stay "Source: to be confirmed" unless someone finds the specific NASA evidence report.

What "checked" means here: the paper exists at that address and its title matches the citation. Nobody has read each paper to confirm that it supports the sentence it sits next to.

Where they are used in the app: every system's drawer on Risks ("Further reading"), and the reading list on Medical status.

## Statements still to be confirmed

Checked on 15 November 2026 for prompt 24. One row for each place in `app/src` that still shows "Source: to be confirmed" (found by searching for `verified: false`, `SourceLine text=""` and "to be confirmed"). Rows are sorted by file. Nothing in the app was changed; where the source says something different from the app, the note says so and a person decides.

How the sources were read:

- NASA pages were opened and read as web pages.
- The two NASA reports are PDFs that the web reader could not decode. Their text was extracted on the machine, and every quote below was matched word for word against that text. "Evidence Report: Risk Factor of Inadequate Nutrition" (240 pages) was read from `https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20150000512/downloads/20150000512.pdf`. The 2022 host-microorganism evidence report was read from `https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/evidence/reports/Host_Micro%20EB_Final%207-6-22.pdf?rnd=0.883247067631803`.
- PubMed's web pages refused automated access (a cookie and bot check). The three PubMed records below were read through NCBI's public E-utilities service (`efetch`, by PubMed number), not on the web page. The address given is the normal PubMed page for the same number.
- "No source found" means: searched, and no page that was opened says it. It does not mean the statement is wrong.

### The six "body of work" entries

Only two of the six are in this repository: the first and third entries of "Key reading" on Medical status (rows above). The other four exist only in the team's design mockup, which is not in the repo, so they could not be looked up here.

### Counts

- Statements found: 15 (14 statements and one reading that shows a value with no claim). The two body-of-work entries in the repo are among them.
- With a source: 7 (gut bacteria change, the intestinal barrier reading, vitamin D, calcium, iron, sodium, omega-3). With none: 8 (mood target, heart rate limit, oxygen limit, condition definition, low-fibre diet, the NASA "altered nutrition and microbiome" title, potassium, heart rate reading).
- Where the source disagrees with or only partly supports the app: calcium (wording), iron (stores return to normal by the end of a 6-month mission), omega-3 (the finding is about fish), potassium (not about a high-salt diet), fibre (a requirement, not a shortfall), and the "altered nutrition and microbiome" title (no such NASA page).
